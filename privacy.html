Respawn Reels Uploader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Respawn Reels Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to post videos to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

What we collect

Only the login tokens TikTok and Google issue when you authorize the app, and basic account info (such as your account name) needed to post. No other personal data is collected.

How it's used and stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and used solely to upload approved videos and check upload status. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to third parties.

Your choices

You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok or Google account settings, which makes the stored tokens useless. The owner can delete them on request.
